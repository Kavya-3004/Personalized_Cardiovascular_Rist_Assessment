### 4. Interpretation of Feature Attributions (SHAP)
Shapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP values) provide local mathematical attributions of the machine learning model's internal feature weightings. They describe statistical correlation within the trained decision tree ensemble and **do not indicate biological causation, pathophysiological mechanism, or diagnostic necessity**.
